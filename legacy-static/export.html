Export Pricing

How export pricing works

VAT-deductible

Marked cars show Germany's 19% VAT separately on the invoice. For a documented export sale, that VAT is zero-rated — so the net price above is what you actually pay.

Margin-taxed (§25a)

Most used cars are sold under Germany's margin scheme: VAT is calculated only on the dealer's profit margin, not the full price, so there's nothing separate to deduct. The listed price is the export price.

Export title

Always confirm the vehicle registration certificate (Fahrzeugbrief) is included before sourcing — a car flagged "No Export Title" generally can't be re-registered abroad.

Prices are indicative export quotes based on current market listings, not final invoices. Shipping, import duties, registration, and any destination-country taxes are additional.
